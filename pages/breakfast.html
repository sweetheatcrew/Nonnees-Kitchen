Sweet Pancakes

Here is a recipe for sweet pancakes.

Ingredients

Pantry/Dry Food
  • Pancake Mix
Wet Ingredients
  • Almond Milk
Seasonings
  • Cinnamon Powder
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Banana Extract
Other
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Butter
  • Optional Toppings: Syrup, Fruit, Whipped Cream, etc.

Equipment

Appliances
  • Stove or Griddle
Standard Tools
  • Stirring Spoon
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Frying Pan
  • Spatula

Instructions

  1. Mix pancake mix and almond milk to a thick, yet runny consistency.
  2. Add splashes of vanilla and banana extract.
  3. Sprinkle in cinnamon powder.
  4. Set your pan to medium-low heat with a teaspoon of vegetable oil.
  5. Add pancake mix to pan when the oil is warm. Create pancakes of your desired size.
  6. Flip pancake with spatula when many bubbles surface, or when the cooking side turns golden brown.
  7. Remove pancake when the other side becomes golden brown.
  8. Smooth butter on top of pancake.
  9. Repeat steps 4-8 for remaining batter.
  10. Serve with your toppings of choice, congratulate yourself, and be fulfilled!

Cheesy Creole Shrimp & Grits

Here is a recipe for Cheesy Creole Shrimp & Grits.

Ingredients

Pantry/Dry Food
  • Grits
Wet Ingredients
  • Heavy Cream
  • Chicken Broth
  • Cheese
  • Butter
Meat
  • Bacon
  • Shrimp
Produce/Vegetables
  • Celery
  • Green Bell Pepper
  • Yellow Onion
  • Garlic
  • For garnish: Green Onions
Seasonings
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Creole Seasoning
  • Parsley Flakes
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
Other
  • Optional Chicken Broth Replacement: Water & Chicken Bouillon

Equipment

Appliances
  • Stove
Standard Tools
  • Stirring Spoon
  • Knife or Slicing Tool
  • Pot
  • Sauce Pan
  • Frying Pan
  • Spatula
  • Optional, but recommended: tongs

Instructions

Grits
  1. Follow instructions on grits package for the correct grits to liquid ratio. Add grits and chicken broth to pot on medium heat.
  2. Add a splash of heavy cream, a pinch of salt, black pepper, and a little bit of butter.
  3. Let your grits cook. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking and burning.
  4. Remove grits from heat when grits have thickened and absorbed the chicken broth.
Cheese Sauce
  1. Chop celery, bell pepper, onion, and garlic.
  2. Heat a saucepan to medium-high heat with butter and oil.
  3. Fry chopped vegetables (except garlic) in a light amount of oil and butter until vegetables start to soften. Then add garlic. Continue frying until vegetables are soft (especially the celery).
  4. Turn temperature down to medium-low heat.
  5. Add heavy cream to saucepan for the base of your sauce.
  6. Season mixture with all seasonings except salt and parsley.
  7. Add a couple handfuls of cheese. Mix and allow cheese to melt and thicken into cream.
  8. Taste and adjust seasonings, if needed.
  9. Remove from heat when sauce reaches desired consistency.
Shrimp & Bacon
  1. Lightly season shrimp with creole seasoning, onion powder, pepper, and parsley flakes.
  2. Heat frying pan to medium heat with light amounts of oil and butter.
  3. Fry shrimp on each side for about 3-4 minutes each.
  4. Remove and set aside when finished.
  5. Chop bacon into small bits.
  6. Fry bacon bits. Remove when finished.
Toppings
  1. In a bowl, layer grits, cheese sauce, shrimp, and bacon bits.
  2. Garnish with green onions and parsley flakes, if desired.
  3. Congratulate yourself and be fulfilled.
