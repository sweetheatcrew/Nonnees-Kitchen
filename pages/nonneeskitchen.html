American Southern Red Beans & Rice

Here is a recipe for classic American Southern Red Beans & Rice.

Ingredients

Pantry/Dry Food
  • Cooked White Rice
  • Red Kidney Beans
  • Chicken Broth
Meat
  • Turkey Part (Of your choice: neck, thighs, wings)
  • Andouille Sausage
Produce/Vegetables
  • Celery
  • Green Bell Pepper
  • Yellow Onion
  • Garlic
  • For garnish: Green Onions
Seasonings
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Creole Seasoning
  • Fresh or Dried Herbs of Choice, or Italian Seasoning
  • Cayenne Pepper
  • Sage
  • Bay Leaves
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
Other
  • Optional Chicken Broth Replacement: Water & Chicken Bouillon
  • Optional: Cornstarch (As Thickening Agent)

Equipment

Appliances
  • Stove or Crockpot
Standard Tools
  • Stirring Spoon
  • Big Pot with Lid
  • Knife or Slicing Tool
  • Bowl
  • Optional, but recommended: tongs
  • For serving: Ladle
Machine Tools
  • Blender or Immersion Blender

Instructions

  1. Soak Red Kidney Beans in water overnight or for at least 4 hours. Drain after.
  2. Lightly season turkey part. Seal it and marinate in fridge.
  3. Chop all vegetables and slice andouille sausage. Keep garlic separate. Keep green onions on side for garnish.
  4. Set your pot to medium-high heat. Fry your sausage in a light amount of oil and butter. You want to brown your sausage. Remove when finished and set aside.
  5. Turn the heat down to medium. Remove excess oil with a paper towel and tool of choice.
  6. Fry chopped vegetables (except garlic) in a light amount of oil and butter until vegetables start to soften. Then add garlic. Continue frying until vegetables are soft. (Optional: add a medium-sized coat of cornstarch at the end. Incorporate with vegetables.)
  7. Add red kidney beans and turkey parts to pot, then add chicken broth.
  8. Season broth.
  9. Turn up the heat until the mixture begins to boil, then turn down the temperature to a low heat. Let cook for 3 hours or until beans are tender.
  10. Use tongs or spoon to remove turkey parts. Shred turkey parts and discard bones and excess fat. Add meat back to the pot.
  11. Gather spoonfuls of beans and set aside in a bowl.
  12. Add andouille sausage to pot.
  13. Blend set-aside beans until creamy. Add blend back to the pot.
  14. Let cook for 5 more minutes, then remove from heat.
  15. Serve on top of white rice, garnish with green onions.
  16. Congratulate yourself and be fulfilled.

Sweet Pancakes

Here is a recipe for sweet pancakes.

Ingredients

Pantry/Dry Food
  • Pancake Mix
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Banana Extract
Wet Ingredients
  • Almond Milk
Seasonings
  • Cinnamon Powder
Other
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Butter
  • Optional Toppings: Syrup, Fruit, Whipped Cream, etc.

Equipment

Appliances
  • Stove or Griddle
Standard Tools
  • Stirring Spoon
  • Mixing Bowl
  • Frying Pan
  • Spatula

Instructions

  1. Mix pancake mix and almond milk to a thick, yet runny consistency.
  2. Add splashes of vanilla and banana extract.
  3. Sprinkle in cinnamon powder.
  4. Set your pan to medium-low heat with a teaspoon of vegetable oil.
  5. Add pancake mix to pan when the oil is warm. Create pancakes of your desired size.
  6. Flip pancake with spatula when many bubbles surface, or when the cooking side turns golden brown.
  7. Remove pancake when the other side becomes golden brown.
  8. Smooth butter on top of pancake.
  9. Repeat steps 4-8 for remaining batter.
  10. Serve with your toppings of choice, congratulate yourself, and be fulfilled!

Cheesy Creole Shrimp & Grits

Here is a recipe for Cheesy Creole Shrimp & Grits.

Ingredients

Pantry/Dry Food
  • Grits
Wet Ingredients
  • Heavy Cream
  • Chicken Broth
  • Cheese
  • Butter
Meat
  • Bacon
  • Shrimp
Produce/Vegetables
  • Celery
  • Green Bell Pepper
  • Yellow Onion
  • Garlic
  • For garnish: Green Onions
Seasonings
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Creole Seasoning
  • Parsley Flakes
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
Other
  • Optional Chicken Broth Replacement: Water & Chicken Bouillon

Equipment

Appliances
  • Stove
Standard Tools
  • Stirring Spoon
  • Knife or Slicing Tool
  • Pot
  • Sauce Pan
  • Frying Pan
  • Spatula
  • Optional, but recommended: tongs

Instructions

Grits
  1. Follow instructions on grits package for the correct grits to liquid ratio. Add grits and chicken broth to pot on medium heat.
  2. Add a splash of heavy cream, a pinch of salt, black pepper, and a little bit of butter.
  3. Let your grits cook. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking and burning.
  4. Remove grits from heat when grits have thickened and absorbed the chicken broth.
Cheese Sauce
  1. Chop celery, bell pepper, onion, and garlic.
  2. Heat a saucepan to medium-high heat with butter and oil.
  3. Fry chopped vegetables (except garlic) in a light amount of oil and butter until vegetables start to soften. Then add garlic. Continue frying until vegetables are soft (especially the celery).
  4. Turn temperature down to medium-low heat.
  5. Add heavy cream to saucepan for the base of your sauce.
  6. Season mixture with all seasonings except salt and parsley.
  7. Add a couple handfuls of cheese. Mix and allow cheese to melt and thicken into cream.
  8. Taste and adjust seasonings, if needed.
  9. Remove from heat when sauce reaches desired consistency.
Shrimp & Bacon
  1. Lightly season shrimp with creole seasoning, onion powder, pepper, and parsley flakes.
  2. Heat frying pan to medium heat with light amounts of oil and butter.
  3. Fry shrimp on each side for about 3-4 minutes each.
  4. Remove and set aside when finished.
  5. Chop bacon into small bits.
  6. Fry bacon bits. Remove when finished.
Toppings
  1. In a bowl, layer grits, cheese sauce, shrimp, and bacon bits.
  2. Garnish with green onions and parsley flakes, if desired.
  3. Congratulate yourself and be fulfilled.
